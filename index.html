Our Journey

Two Decades of Steel Excellence

From a small trading desk to one of the region's most trusted steel distributors.

2006

The Beginning

Kuber Associates was founded with a vision to bridge the gap between steel manufacturers and industrial buyers, starting with secondary steel trading in retail and B2B segments.

2010

Expansion & Growth

Expanded operations with dedicated warehouse facilities and became an authorized distributor of HR products, serving fabrication, tube manufacturing, and cold rolling industries.

2016

Regional Leadership

Grew exponentially across Rajasthan and NCR, establishing long-term partnerships with major steel mills and building a team of 40+ industry experts.

2020

TATA Steel Partnership

Became an distributor for TATA Steel Ltd., strengthening our product portfolio with premium-grade HR coils, sheets, and plates.

2024

50,000+ MT Milestone

Crossed 50,000 metric tons in cumulative deliveries, continuing to power India's manufacturing, infrastructure, and fabrication sectors.
