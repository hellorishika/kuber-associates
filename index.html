About us

A trusted name in Jaipur steel

20+Years in the steel industry

Kuber Associates is a leading iron and steel merchant and supplier based in Jaipur. Since 2006 we have supplied sheets and plates to fabricators, contractors, workshops and manufacturers across the region — committed, throughout, to delivering quality products and services.

Customers trust us because we keep our word. The gauge we commit to is the gauge we deliver. When the market moves sharply against us, we honour the price we quoted rather than passing the shock on. And we deal with every buyer fairly, whether the order is a single sheet or a full truckload — which is how we have earned a reputation across the Jaipur trade as a solid, committed supplier.
